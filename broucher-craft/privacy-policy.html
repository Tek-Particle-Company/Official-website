Privacy Policy

BrochureCraft — Android App

This Privacy Policy explains how BrochureCraft, an Android brochure and menu design app built by Tek Particle, handles information on your device. BrochureCraft is designed as a local-first app: your designs, brand assets, and preferences are stored on your own device using local SQLite (Room) storage, not on our servers.

1. Information We Collect

BrochureCraft does not require an account and does not transmit your designs, brand kit, images, or contact details to any Tek Particle server. All content you create — designs, templates, brand colors, logos, and contact information — is stored locally in the app's designs, templates, and brand_kit database tables on your device, along with onboarding and plan preferences saved via local DataStore Preferences.

The app may access device permissions you explicitly grant, such as storage/media access (to import logos or images) and file sharing (to export or share a finished PDF, PNG, or JPG). These permissions are used solely to perform the action you request and are not used to collect or transmit personal data.

2. How Information Is Used

Any information you enter — business type, brand identity, contact details, and canvas content — is used only to render your brochures and menus within the app and to populate exports (PDF/PNG/JPG) that you generate and share yourself via the Android share sheet.

3. Local Storage, No Network Requirement

BrochureCraft's canvas editor, template gallery, brand kit, and export tools function entirely offline. The app does not require an internet connection to create, edit, save, or export designs, and no design data is uploaded to the cloud.

4. Premium / PRO Unlock

The Premium Upgrade screen persists a local "PRO" flag using DataStore Preferences to unlock PRO-gated templates and features on your device. This release does not integrate a payment or billing SDK, and no payment information is collected or transmitted by the app.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Because BrochureCraft does not send your designs or personal data to Tek Particle or any third party, we do not sell, share, or disclose your content. If a future version integrates optional cloud backup, analytics, or billing services, this Policy will be updated in advance to describe what is collected and why.

6. Data Security

Your data's security depends on your device's own protections (screen lock, encryption, backups). We recommend keeping your device and OS updated, and using Android's built-in backup/restore options if you wish to preserve your designs across devices.

7. Children's Privacy

BrochureCraft is a general-purpose design tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, as the app does not collect personal information from any user in the first place.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as BrochureCraft evolves (for example, if cloud sync, analytics, or in-app purchases are added in a future release). Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how BrochureCraft handles data on your device, please contact us through our contact page.
