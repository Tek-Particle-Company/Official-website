Privacy Policy

Home / Privacy Policy

At Tek Particle, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal and business information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and protect information in connection with our business solutions, software products, consulting services, marketing services, and other technology offerings.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, phone number, company name, role, project requirements, billing details, and contact preferences. We also collect information generated through our websites, app experiences, client portals, and email communications.

In addition, we may collect technical data such as IP address, browser type, device information, page visits, referral sources, cookies, analytics data, and usage statistics related to our software, platforms, and marketing websites.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and improve our end-to-end business services, including software development, product strategy, UI/UX design, marketing activation, business planning, consulting, automation, and technology implementation.

We may also use information to respond to inquiries, manage proposals and contracts, send service updates, maintain client records, improve platform performance, support app operations, protect against fraud, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Our Apps and Software

Tek Particle offers software products and mobile app experiences that may collect usage metrics, app performance information, crash data, and account data needed to provide functionality and maintain product quality. We may process user data for support, analytics, security, and continuous improvement of the services we deliver.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal data. We may share information with trusted service providers that support our operations, such as hosting providers, analytics tools, payment processors, CRM systems, marketing vendors, customer support tools, and security partners. These third parties are only allowed to process data under strict confidentiality obligations and for legitimate business purposes.

We may disclose information when required by law, to protect our legal rights, to prevent fraud or abuse, to address security incidents, or as needed to fulfill a contract or service request.

5. Cookies and Analytics

Our websites may use cookies, pixels, and similar technologies to improve site performance, understand visitor behavior, and personalize experiences. You can adjust your browser settings to disable cookies, though some features of our site may not function properly without them.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, comply with legal requirements, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, update, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal information. You may also withdraw consent where consent is used as the legal basis for processing. To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information below.

9. International Transfers

If you access our services from outside the country where we operate, your information may be transferred to and processed in other jurisdictions where data protection laws may differ. We will use appropriate safeguards as required by applicable law.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us at business@tekparticle.online or through our contact page.

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with the updated effective date.
